# ADR-033 — Standardise InvertSpreadToPrice to MathExp(T) Across All Branches

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The three SELL branches in InvertSpreadToPrice used MathExp(-T),
a legacy artifact from when the matrix fired aggressive market orders
based on the triad directional vector. After ADR-032 decoupled Option A
quoting to explicitly construct bid_spread < offer_spread, the negative
exponent in SELL branches inverted the price relationship, forcing
Price_buy > Price_sell and collapsing bracket width to zero. ADR-013
then crushed the crossed bracket to a 4-pip box at top-of-book.

## Decision

Strip the minus sign from all three SELL branch MathExp(-T)
calls. All six branches now uniformly evaluate MathExp(T). With
bid_spread always less than offer_spread (per ADR-032), MathExp(T)
guarantees Price_bid < Price_offer for all signal magnitudes and
directions.

## Consequences

Bracket geometry restored. Symmetry check produces
~9.2 pip bracket for flat signal with QuoteSpread=0.0004. ADR-013
passivity pre-check (ADR-032) protects already-passive distant quotes
from being dragged to top-of-book. No change to Option B, exit logic,
carry engine, or any other component.
