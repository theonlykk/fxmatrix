# ADR-031 — Amputate Marketable Reversion Market Hedge — Strict Passive Exit

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The Marketable Reversion Exception fired market hedge orders
when IsPassive() rejected a passive exit limit. With QuoteSpread=0.0004
and tight exit targets, IsPassive() fails frequently, causing the EA to
continuously cross the spread with aggressive market orders. This
destroys the mathematical edge of a passive market maker. ADR-029's
state lock treated the symptom; this ADR treats the cause.

## Decision

Remove the market hedge OrderSend path entirely. When
exit_price < 0.0 (IsPassive rejected), log and return. The EA retries
on every subsequent HandleEntryFill call until the market drifts far
enough for a passive limit to be placed. ADR-032 will implement the
Dynamic Exit Clamp to eliminate this wait entirely.

## Consequences

EA is strictly passive on exits. No market orders ever
fired by the exit engine. Positions held until passive exit is possible.
Spread edge preserved on every trade. ADR-029 hedge_in_flight lock
removed as it is no longer needed.
