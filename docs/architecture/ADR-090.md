# ADR-090: Add-Spacing Compression (Shallow/Mid-Layer Capital Efficiency)

## Status
Approved (Gemini Staff Architect ruling) — Parameters finalized, pending DeepSeek review

## Context
Investigation into the exit/add-spacing asymmetry (see prior findings: exit
distance structurally pinned near the 3-pip MinLayerExitPoints floor
regardless of layer depth, due to the E_n = effective_spread * PHI^(layer+1)
term shrinking with depth since PHI≈0.618<1; add-spacing meanwhile grows
exponentially via GridExpBase=1.5 from layer 3 onward) revealed that
MaxLayers=20 is essentially never reached in practice — worst case observed
across all backtests to date was layer 6, roughly 25% of designed capacity.

Two candidate fixes were identified: (1) tighten add-spacing so more of the
designed layer capacity is actually usable in normal conditions, or (2)
directly address the exponential growth rate itself (GridExpBase). This ADR
covers (1); the companion re-architecture of GridExpBase is tracked
separately (pending).

## Decision
DebugEnableAddSpacingCompression, AddSpacingMultiplier, and
MinAddDistancePoints (shipped in commit a630782) are set as the new
compiled defaults:

- DebugEnableAddSpacingCompression = true (was false)
- AddSpacingMultiplier = 0.5 (was 1.0)
- MinAddDistancePoints = 90 (unchanged — 9 pips)

Applied in ComputeNextLayerPrice() as A_n_final = max(MinAddDistancePoints
in price units, A_n_original * AddSpacingMultiplier) — scales and floors the
final combined add-distance regardless of which internal term
(base_add/E_n+10pt/kinetic_dist) produced it.

## Empirical validation
2-week backtest (2026.05.18–06.01), all three instances (MM_LONG,
MM_SHORT, BIAS_BOTH), sensitivity sweep at 0.75x/0.5x/0.33x against the
3-pip baseline:

| Instance | Baseline P&L | 0.5x P&L | Improvement | Stuck positions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MM_LONG | $30.50 | $53.69 | +76.0% | 0 (unchanged) |
| MM_SHORT | $24.24 | $31.55 | +30.2% | 1 (unchanged — pre-existing GBPUSD episode, resolves favorably at account level per prior analysis) |
| BIAS_BOTH | $50.66 | $55.56 | +9.7% | 0 (unchanged) |

Benefit is monotonic across the full 0.75x/0.5x/0.33x range tested, with no
plateau or reversal — 0.33x outperforms 0.5x on every instance (MM_LONG
+129.0%, MM_SHORT +69.0%, BIAS_BOTH +16.9%). 0.5x was selected over 0.33x
specifically as a conservative choice given GridExpBase's exponential
growth remains unaddressed (see Consequences) — not because 0.5x maximizes
backtested P&L. Win rates remained stable (97.4-98.9%) across all
configurations tested; the mechanism generates larger/more frequent
resolutions rather than trading win rate for size.

MM_LONG (no directional flip capability) showed the largest relative
benefit at every compression level tested, consistent with tighter
add-spacing mattering most for instances with no alternative escape from an
adverse trend.

## Consequences
- This is a shallow/mid-layer (roughly layers 0-6) capital-efficiency
  improvement. It does not address why layers ~10-20 remain effectively
  unreachable — that requires directly bounding GridExpBase's exponential
  growth, tracked as a separate, larger architectural change.
- Chosen deliberately conservative (0.5x rather than the empirically
  stronger 0.33x) pending that separate fix, to avoid combining an
  aggressive shallow-layer optimization with an already-unbounded deep-layer
  growth curve.
- No change to exit-side logic (MinLayerExitPoints remains 3 pips) or to
  ComputeGridInterval()/ComputeKineticDistance() internals — this
  compresses their combined output, it doesn't alter how they compute their
  individual terms.
