# ADR-048 — Cooldown Drag Freeze Level Clamp

**Status:** Committed

## Context

`RunSpreadCooldownReconciliation()` drag was universally blocked by `IsClearOfFreezeLevel()` — 4.5 hours of failed attempts in 2026-06-26 session. `ideal_price` computed by signal engine consistently fell inside broker freeze zone, causing silent `continue` on every bar.

## Decision

Replace skip-on-freeze with clamp-to-freeze-boundary. Compute `min_dist = freeze_dist + 2 * point`, clamp `ideal_price` to minimum legal distance from market, then pass through `IsClearOfFreezeLevel()` as final safety gate.

## Consequences

Cooldown contraction now executes as designed; orders dragged to tightest legally permitted position; `IsClearOfFreezeLevel()` retained as defence-in-depth post-clamp.

## Negative Space

`IsClearOfFreezeLevel()` function unchanged; entry/exit limit call sites in `ExecutionEngine.mqh` unchanged; no new parameters.
