## 1. ONE WORKED EXAMPLE (why the grid does not earn)

EURUSD OPT, add = exit = 7 pips, cap 7 (`research/longrun/results.md` s1). In a range the stack fills and exits over
and over: each close +7 pips, $0.70 less $0.06. Then price falls 60 pips and does not come back that week: the long
side fills to the cap, and each further 7 pips rolls the deepest layer down. When price bounces, a rolled layer
closes at a loss of tens of pips (40 pips = -$4.00). Thirty small wins pay for one roll. Over four years EURUSD OPT
scalped +$22,096 and paid -$23,314 in rolled closes. Close the book every night instead and the same losers are paid
as cut closes, sooner and dearer (s3).

## 3. WHY: WHAT THE GRID IS

- **A short-gamma book on a near random walk.** The operator's own reading (24 Oct, `research/t3_drawdown/results.md`):
  "we are seeing the expected results of running a short gamma strategy on a random walk ... squeezing the limited
  'non random walk' part of the mkt action out - but that is eaten away by commission". The four years measure it:
  scalps and rolled losses nearly cancel on every pair; before commission the twelve made +$1,087 in 46 months.
- **"we are not really earning, we are just monetizing mtm winners" (the operator, 31 Oct): confirmed on the
  winners.** His other half, that losses wait in the book to turn into winners, is not how they arrive
  (`research/longrun/results.md` s4): losers are held for days to weeks and realised through rolls on the partial
  bounce, in fewer and larger pieces than the wins.
- **Overnight holding is not the P&L problem, and removing it makes things worse** (`research/nocarry/results.md`
  s2): flat every night, the grid scalps more (+$15k, a fresh straddle at every 01:00 restart) but turns $79k of
  roll losses into $108k of cut losses. Before commission DAY is -$11,487. Overnight, carried layers were winning
  part of it back.
- **No price structure for it to harvest:** two pre-registered tests found no reversion at round numbers (Osler) and
  no pull a grid can use (magnet). With the four years: three angles, one reading.

  No account rule makes it both safe and profitable.

## 5. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY (so the conclusion is not stretched)

- It tests the **symmetric passive grid** as built: OPT and ALT arms, add = exit, cap 7, the lattice and re-roll, at
  E's t3 widths. sweep9-11 covered the add / exit surface on October's ticks; the four years cover t3 only. A
  different premise (a directional filter, a signal deciding when the grid may trade) is a new project, not a
  geometry of this one; nothing here argues for or against one.
- **Bars, not ticks** over the four years (checked against the tick replay: +$180 against +$231 over about the same
  weeks, s2); **no swap** anywhere (it would lower CONT and WEEK further); one feed (FTMO's); and the other limits of
  the bar simulator in `research/nocarry/PREREG.md` s7 (the bars' spread field, holiday weeks skipped, no rollover
  pause).
- **Not run over four years:** exit different from add, cap 9 (s2), or any geometry but t3.
- **A desk-style rule** (adds pulled at the close, exits left working overnight) sits between DAY and CONT and was not
  run. Both neighbours lose; nothing suggests a hybrid of two losing rules earns. The no-carry runner does most of
  it, if the operator wants it pre-registered and closed.
- It does not say the work was wasted: s7.

## 7. WHAT THE PROJECT LEAVES (factual; no claim of value beyond it)

- **A validated tick replay** of the EA's mechanics, fit for count-level use on EURUSD outside scheduled-release bursts
  (`replay-plan4`: T1 97.3-100% on the seen windows, 98.2-99.2% on the unseen one, at timing 1;
  `docs/research/replay-calibration-eurusd-4.md`); its P&L is a guide outside those counts. And a minute-bar
  simulator checked against it (`research/opt_budget/sim.py`, `research/opt_budget/MECHANICS.md`).
- **A research method that caught its own errors:** pre-registration before code, tests before runners, self-checks
  before results. It caught the bar-fill flaw (+$33,162 of false profit, withdrawn 31 Oct) and v3.1 of the Osler test
  (a tilted comparison, fixed before any real number was read).
- **An operational record:** four fleets on Wine VPSs, telemetry and reconciliation (pipshed), FTMO's request limit
  solved (C165: a sixty-fold drop at the 1 Nov open), and three trial records (two in `docs/FULL_TRIAL_RECORD_*.md`,
  E's in HANDOFF s93).
