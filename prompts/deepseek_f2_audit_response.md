## T-1 — Order inside the sync step

**Verdict: NEEDS-FIX.**

The reset’s REMOVEs and the completion’s PLACEs are not double-logged: `Rpl_SyncCompleteCloseByAt` calls `Rpl_TmProcessSyncRemovals(sync_tick_ms)` first, then after the completion calls `Rpl_TmDiffStageSends(...)`, and the later `Rpl_TmProcessSyncRemovals(t)` sees the updated snapshot. No stale market seed survives into a later stage: in timed mode `Rpl_TmBrokerStep` uses the passed `bid/ask`, and `Rpl_TmEaStepAtTick` re-seeds; in timing 0 the caller immediately does `Grind_MarketTestSeed(tick.bid, tick.ask, 0, 0);`.
