PREMISE VERDICT  -- is it safe to enable carry? one paragraph

No — carry is **not safe to enable** until T-3 is fixed. The carry pass writes the modified exit price to the broker first, then commits accrued and shift to global variables. A crash, terminal kill, or restart between those two steps leaves a live exit order that reflects the new carry-aware price while the GVs still hold the old accrued and shift. On the next reconstruction, `Grind_ReconExitMatchesEntry` compares the broker price against the stale GVs, I6 fails, and the instance halts. Every other threat (T-1, T-2, T-4–T-9) holds under the current code, but this single interruption window is enough to make enabling carry unsafe in production. The smallest safe fix is a recovery step in `Grind_RebuildBookFromTicketsInner` that detects this specific mismatch, recomputes accrued from the live position swap, derives the implied shift from the actual order price, and updates the GVs before re-checking I6.
