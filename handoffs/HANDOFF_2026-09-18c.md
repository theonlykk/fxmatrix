## 3. WHY K=1 IS SAFE -- NOW MEASURED, NOT ARGUED

The exit queue ranks by entry price NEAREST TO MARKET. On a ladder built
downward the nearest is the DEEPEST, newest layer, and a new add fill becomes
the new rank 0. **K=1 always rests the exit most likely to fill.** Only rank
1 is forfeited.

I framed this to Gemini backwards -- as sacrificing "deep exit immediacy" --
and he ruled on that twice, approving then overturning. The corrected
geometry is what reinstated K=1.

**Losing rank 1 is cheaper than it looks.** `Grind_ExitQClampPassive`
(`grind_exitq.mqh:242`) routes to `Grind_CarryClampLongExit`
(`grind_carry.mqh:443`), which places a late exit at `ask + min_dist` when
the target has been passed. Price moved past the target, so that is a BETTER
fill than the formula. The only losing case is a two-level spike that reverts
before the next tick.

The operator predicted the clamp before it was checked. Gemini asserted MT5
would fill a passed held-exit target with positive slippage -- wrong, because
a held exit has no order at the broker at all.

Confirmed by measurement at 15:13:32 on the desktop:
`req0=true req1=false cover2=true`. I3 required coverage at rank 0 only.

`Grind_ExitQFormulaTarget(1.10300, 3.0, 0.00001, true) = 1.10303`. Wrong:
`Grind_PipsToPrice` is `pips * point * 10.0` (`grind_pure.mqh:65`), so the

suite -- it has no MetaEditor -- so the reason string was an inference
presented as an observation.**

**Mine, twice.** First that a leaked carry shift would be cleared by
`Grind_CarryTestReset()`. It clears in-memory harness arrays; the shift is a
GlobalVariable. Then, after that failed, I was forming a third theory on the
same unverified chain.

**The measurement settled it in one line.** A temporary `Print` gave:

    reason=I6_LONG_EXIT shift=0.045250000000000234
    expected=1.1033 target=1.1033 cover2=true req0=true req1=false

4,525 points of carry shift on position 5003.
`Grind_ReconExitMatchesEntry` computes `expected = formula + shift`, so
`diff = -0.04525` against a `2 * point` tolerance. Fixed with three
`Grind_CarryShiftDelete()` calls, which remove both the shift and release
GVs.

**Lesson: when two confident diagnoses have failed, instrument. Do not form
a third.** One `Print` cost one round trip and produced six facts.
