- **FTMO's rule** (ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives, 2-Step): EQUITY must stay
  above (BALANCE at 00:00 CE(S)T - 5% of initial capital, fixed $500).
  The anchor is BALANCE: floating loss carried over FTMO midnight counts
  against the new day from its first second (~$110 headroom after a ~$390
  floating book). Closing a loss BEFORE midnight leaves today's equity
  unchanged and restores tomorrow's headroom -- at the cost of the
  inventory's recovery. FTMO's day turns at 01:00 BROKER time.

  GMT with the EU DST rule; anchor = balance minus EVERY deal (any type)

  silently disable the breaker); allowance = 5% of the first deposit (no
