## 1. ROLL-AT-CAP (operator idea) -- STATUS: SIM ONLY

Problem: at cap a ladder cannot add; once price passes the deepest layer by
one add step the side is stranded and cannot scalp until price returns.
Idea: close the oldest (most underwater) layer to keep a foothold at price.

Agreed economics:
- Exposure unchanged (P layers). If price never returns, roll dominates
  stall (losses real either way; scalps are extra).
- If price returns, stall recovers all layers; roll loses the closed ones.
  Each roll realises ~P x add_pips (~80 pips GBPUSD at 8 x 10) + spread +
  commission: break-even ~P scalps per foothold in the reversal case.
- Carry: rolling bounds holding time (oldest layers carry most financing).
- Spacing: rolling removes the trend-length bet built into add x cap; spacing
  becomes "scalp density vs roll cost". May SUPERSEDE ALT progressive
  widening -- state this in any ADR.
- Frame: a forced close is a stop loss and crosses the spread; both forbidden
  by ARCHITECT s1 and ADR-125. Needs an ADR superseding both if adopted.
- Order purgatory still required: rolling keeps trending ladders at cap; at
  16 instances x P=8, held exits remain arithmetic necessity (all-exits-rest
  bound: P<=4 at 16 instances, P<=6 at 12).

**Gemini ruling:** operator's request to skip backtest OVERRULED; simulator
