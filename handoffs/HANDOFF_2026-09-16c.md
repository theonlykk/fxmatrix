## 1. WHAT WAS DECIDED

**ADR-151 ACCEPTED** (`docs/architecture/ADR-151-order-purgatory.md`, 111
lines). Read it; this section is only the shape.

  - **Exit queue.** Per side, rank layers by entry price nearest to market
    (longs ascending, shorts descending, tie lower layer_index). Ranks < K
    must have a live exit; ranks < K+H may; beyond are HELD and released as
    front exits fill and CloseBy nets them. Operator's insight: exits further
    back cannot fill until nearer ones do.
  - **Commitment guard.** Exits need `free >= 1`. Entries need
    `free - resting_ent >= 2 + margin`, sent inside a fleet GV lock with
    10 s staleness recovery, one entry send per instance per tick.
  - **Constants (compiled):** K=2, H=1, margin 4, lock stale 10000 ms.
  - **Invariants:** I3 coverage only for required ranks; I1 and I6 only for
    layers with an exit; held layers get formula `exit_target`, never 0.0.
  - **Release pricing:** formula, clamped passive; a clamped price stores its
    shift plus a release-marker GV that exempts it from the carry bound (I6
    strict).
  - **Trim before protect:** OnInit after reconstruction, quarantine branch,
    engine tick start. **Halt cancels own entries** at every halt site.
  - **Phase A = carry off** (all 18 presets already false; OnInit refuses if on).
    Phase B (carry-aware targets, promotion, deadband 10 pips) later.

**Capacity arithmetic** (operator-derived): per instance one-sided worst case
is `P + K + H + 1`; fleet `2N(P + K + H + 1) <= 200 - margin`. Today's 14
instances fit at P=8. **Majors at P=12 do not.** P 12 -> 8 is a follow-on ADR
(Gemini Q6), with the I7 migration trap.
