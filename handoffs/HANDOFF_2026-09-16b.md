## 2. FINDINGS

**F1. The mechanism from 09-16 reproduces at scale.** Five halts in three
minutes from a book at 198. Twelve distinct halt events by 18:46Z.

**F2. A halted instance's resting entries keep filling.** The EA stops, the
broker does not. Fills after a halt create positions the tracker never sees
(absent from `layers`) with no exit. Observed twice at 18:02. **This corrects
a claim made in-session that halts fail safe because exposure stops growing.**

**F3. A halted instance's exits also keep filling.** Each becomes a locked
hedged pair holding 2 slots. Harmless. Reconstruction nets them on reinit --
observed three times, each banking the scalp.

**F4. Reinit cannot heal a naked layer.** Reconstruction treats a position
without exit coverage as `I3_*_NAKED` and fails (RECON_FAIL), per the
"halt, don't repair" rule. A manually placed exit does not help: terminal
orders carry magic 0 and reconstruction ignores them. **The only remedy is to
close the naked position by hand, then reinit.**

**F5. Reinit at a full book re-halts within minutes.** 3 of 3 at 18:38-18:42.
A reinit re-places entries, which take the slots the next exit needs. When no
slot is free, entries are simply refused (AUDCAD OPT 18:23: running, no
entries, no halt).

**F6. Refused orders are retried per tick: a request storm.** Counter
822 -> 3,414 in 23 minutes, about 2,500 of it in 20. It stopped within
minutes of parking. FTMO's EA hyperactivity rule is reported as 2,000
server requests per day (third-party summaries; **confirm against FTMO's own
rules**). Many attempts were refused locally by the terminal
(`duration_ms=0`); **whether FTMO counts those is unknown**, as is the
consequence on a demo account.

**F7. Parking is the only stable state at a full book.** Halted + entries
deleted + naked closed = zero requests, no new exposure, exits still working.

**F8. Status after a halt is stale in `layers`.** A halted instance stops
updating its tracker, so closed tickets still appear under `layers`. **Read
`book.positions` and `book.orders` for truth.** Two false "ticket still open"
calls were made this way at 18:49-18:50.

**F9. Counters.** `scalps` resets to 0 on reinit; `realised_pnl_today`
persisted. Manual closes and manual Close By do not enter EA realised.

**F10. Unexplained, low priority.** EURUSD OPT L05 filled at 1.15023 at

## 4. PROCEDURES

### P1. Park a halted instance

1. From `book.orders`, **delete every ENT order** on that instance. Do this
   first: they are the only thing that can add untracked exposure.
2. From `book.positions`, find positions with no exit (the halt reason names
   the side; untracked fills will not be in `layers`). **Close them.**
3. Leave locked pairs (ENT position + EXT position) alone.
4. Do NOT reinit while the book is near 200.
5. Confirm with a status read: no ENT orders on that instance.

Match on ticket always. GBPUSD and EURUSD carry dozens of positions at
similar prices.

### P2. Recover parked instances (quiet market only)

1. AccountLimits. Proceed only below about **170**.
2. Reinit **one** instance (append a visible character to `InpConfigWarning`;
   a trailing space may not register).
3. Status read: `halted: false`, `recon_ok: true`, entries present on both
   sides, locked pair gone.
4. AccountLimits again before the next one.
5. Start with instances holding locked pairs (AUDCHF OPT, CADCHF OPT): their
   reinit frees 2 slots each.

## 5. DESIGN IMPLICATIONS -- for the exit-priority memo

Operator's name for the idea: **order purgatory** -- entries wait off-book when
the account is near its limit; only exits get real slots.

Evidence this session gives the memo:

1. Exits must never be refused for want of a slot. Reserve headroom, or cancel
   the instance's own furthest entry to make room (F1).
2. **On halt, the EA should cancel its own resting entries** (F2). This alone
   would have removed both untracked fills and most manual work.
3. Refused sends need backoff, not per-tick retry (F6).
4. Reinit should not re-place entries into a book above a threshold (F5).
5. Open question, not a proposal: should reconstruction ever place a missing
   exit when a slot exists? That contradicts "halt, don't repair" (F4) and
   needs a Gemini ruling.
6. Layer cap: GBPUSD ALT reached 12/12. Supports the cap-lowering discussion
   in `HANDOFF_2026-09-16.md` s7.
