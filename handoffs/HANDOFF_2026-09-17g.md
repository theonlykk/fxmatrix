## 2. THREE BUGS CAUGHT IN REVIEW (all fixed on branch)

B1. `near_market` used `add_pips * _Point`; the codebase converts pips with
    `Grind_PipsToPrice` = `pips * point * 10.0`. The band was 1 pip instead of
    one add step, so nearly every add would have been classed FAR and blocked by
    the reserve -- the opposite of the ADR's intent. Now
    `Grind_AddTargetNearMarket` (grind_engine.mqh ~766).

    placement without setting the due flag: neither placed nor retried. Now sets
    the due flag when `use_try_lock` is true.

    broker UTC+3). Now `Grind_MarketTimeMsc()` (`SYMBOL_TIME_MSC`) minus the
    deal's msc, floored at 0, seedable in tests.
